# SourceTrace design world

Reading this as a trust-critical editorial workbench for non-lawyers. Variance 4, motion 2, density 4. Inspired by a marked-up legal brief: bone paper, near-black ink, rust correction marks, thin rules, oversized serif title, mono provenance labels. The transformation from input to claim ledger is the central visual. No cards, gradients, or faux AI sparkle. Status uses text and shape as well as color. Reduced motion removes reveal transitions.

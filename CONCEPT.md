# SourceTrace

**One line:** An inspectable second opinion on AI-generated debt collection guidance.

Paste an answer. SourceTrace splits it into claims, compares explicit statements against four curated federal FDCPA rules, and gives each claim a linked source and a narrow explanation. Anything outside that coverage is marked for human review. The app runs entirely in the browser and stores nothing.

**Audience:** People who received a confident AI answer about a debt collector, and legal-aid staff who need to triage it. **Track:** AI Safety, Ethics & Governance. **Boundary:** U.S. federal FDCPA provisions only; this is information, not legal advice or a universal verifier.
